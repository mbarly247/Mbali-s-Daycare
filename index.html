About Little Angels

A caring start for a bright future.

Little Angels is a welcoming daycare and early learning centre in Northgate, Randburg. We create a secure, joyful and stimulating environment where every child can explore, build confidence and develop at their own pace.

Our dedicated teachers combine caring routines with age-appropriate preschool learning, creative play, outdoor activity and opportunities for social development.
